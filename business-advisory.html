Business Advisory

Strategic Guidance for Sustainable Growth. Success requires more than compliance—it demands informed decisions and forward-thinking strategies. Our advisory services are designed to help businesses improve financial performance, manage risks, and unlock new opportunities for growth.

Our Scope of Practice

Strategic Financial Direction & Leadership

Empowering executive leadership with forward-looking financial intelligence, capital structuring, and strategic CFO counsel.

Business Structuring & Entity Selection

Evaluating legal options (Proprietorship vs Partnership vs LLP vs Private Limited) based on tax liability, founder liability, investor requirements, and future fundraising roadmap.

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)

Rigorous financial modeling, break-even analysis, unit economics evaluation, gross margin optimization, and operational variance reporting comparing actual performance to target budgets.

Startup Advisory & Business Setup

End-to-end founding counsel — DPIIT Startup India registration, angel tax exemption filing, pitch deck financial modeling, cap table management, and ESOP scheme drafting.

Investment Planning

Capital structure advice evaluating debt vs equity funding, bank CMA credit report generation, working capital limit enhancement, and investor due diligence preparation.

Budgeting & Forecasting

Formulating annual operating budgets, departmental expense caps, revenue growth projections, and scenario planning (best case, expected case, stress case) to maintain fiscal discipline.

Cash Flow Management

Designing 13-week rolling cash flow projections, working capital cycle optimization, inventory turnover acceleration, and receivable collection strategies to maintain high liquidity.

Virtual CFO & Strategic Advisory Services

Part-time executive CFO leadership for growing companies. We participate in monthly board meetings, oversee corporate finance, direct banking relationships, and ensure strategic financial alignment.
