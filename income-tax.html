Our Scope of Practice

Comprehensive Tax Advisory & Filing Solutions

We provide personalized tax solutions for individuals, firms, companies, startups, and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs).

Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing

Accurate, timely preparation and e-filing of ITR-1 through ITR-7 for salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, partnerships, LLPs, Private Limited Companies, trusts, and NRIs, ensuring full disclosure and zero penalty risks.

Tax Planning & Optimization

Strategic tax planning under legal provisions (Sections 80C, 80D, 80G, HRA, LTA, Chapter VI-A deductions, and New vs Old Tax Regime comparison) to lawfully minimize your tax liability and maximize post-tax earnings.

Income Tax Assessments

Comprehensive assistance during formal Income Tax proceedings. We review your books, prepare reconciliation statements, compile supporting documentation, and represent your case before Assessing Officers.

Tax Notice & Scrutiny Representation

Professional representation before Income Tax authorities for notices under Sections 142(1), 143(1), 143(2), 148, or 156. We draft detailed, legally sound formal replies to resolve departmental queries smoothly.

Capital Gains Tax Advisory

Specialized tax structuring for real estate transactions, stock market gains, mutual funds, crypto assets, and business sales, utilizing exemptions under Sections 54, 54F, 54EC to optimize capital gains liabilities.

Advance Tax Planning & Compliance

Quarterly income estimation and precise advance tax calculations (due 15th June, Sept, Dec, March) to prevent mandatory interest penalties under Sections 234B and 234C.
