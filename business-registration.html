Business Registration

Build Your Business on a Strong Foundation. Every successful enterprise begins with the right legal and regulatory framework. At J D AHYA & CO., we simplify the business registration process, ensuring a smooth, compliant, and hassle-free start for your entrepreneurial journey.

Our Scope of Practice

Entity Incorporation & Statutory Setup

Complete assistance for selecting entity structures, MCA filing, taxation allocations, and government license procurement.

Proprietorship Registration

Fast-track setup for sole business owners, including MSME/Udyam registration, GST registration, bank account opening documentation, and Professional Tax enrollment.

Partnership Firm Registration

Drafting legally binding Partnership Deeds, notarization, registration with Registrar of Firms (ROF), PAN allocation, TAN setup, and partnership bank account authorization.

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Registration

Complete LLP formation under Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) — RUN-LLP name reservation, FiLLiP incorporation forms, Form 3 LLP agreement drafting, and Certificate of Incorporation (COI).

Private Limited Company Incorporation

End-to-end SPICe+ MCA incorporation — Name approval, Digital Signature Certificates (DSC), Director Identification Numbers (DIN), e-MOA, e-AOA drafting, Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, TAN, and EPFO/ESIC registrations.

MSME (Udyam) Registration

Official Udyam portal registration for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises to unlock collateral-free bank loans, delayed payment protection (MSMED Act), interest subsidies, and government tender benefits.

PAN & TAN Registration

Filing Form 49A / 49B for fast allotment of Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) required for tax compliance and mandatory business deductions.

Professional Tax (PT) Registration

Enrollment Certificate (PTEC) for business owners and Registration Certificate (PTRC) for deducting professional tax from employee salaries as per Gujarat State Tax rules.

Import Export Code (IEC) Registration

10-digit IEC allotment from Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) mandatory for commercial import and export of goods or international service trade from India.
